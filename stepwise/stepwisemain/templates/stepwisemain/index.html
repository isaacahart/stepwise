Welcome to Stepwise!

Stepwise lets you prove mathematical theorems using code blocks. You don't need to have any background in math to use Stepwise - it is intended to be accessible to anyone who is curious about proofs.

Learning proofs shouldn't be a tedious task. That's why in Stepwise, proofs are structured like a game, where in each level you prove a theorem that builds off of what you proved in previous levels. These "games" are created by users just like you. And you can create your own projects for others to play, themed around whatever you like! You're encouraged to come up with your own mathematical systems (or bring real-world theories into Stepwise) and share them with the Stepwise community. And don't worry... all such creations are worthwhile!

Where to start

New to Stepwise? Try the tutorial to learn the basic functionality. Then check out some of the projects created by other users.
Once you're a bit familiar with Stepwise, you can create an account and begin defining and proving your own theorems.

Help improve Stepwise

Stepwise is still very young, so it needs your help to grow!
One way to help is to create and share projects.
Another way is to suggest features or report bugs. I'm especially interested in ideas for how to make Stepwise more approachable, since I understand how tricky these highly abstract concepts can be.
